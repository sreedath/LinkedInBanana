How can India be ready to build foundational level LLM/ AI models?

I recorded a 3 hour workshop to demonstrate how to build a completely functional Small Language Model (SLM) from scratch.

You can access it on our Youtube channel here: https://lnkd.in/gVUB3hr2

After this workshop, you will be equipped to build any Small Language Model (SLM) of your choice!

The video already has a large number of views since it was released yesterday.

My only hope and desire is that everyone learns to build SLMs from scratch.

Here is what I cover in the workshop:

(a) Download a dataset with 1million+ samples
(b) Pre-process and tokenize the dataset
(c) Divide the dataset into input-target pairs
(d) Assemble the SLM architecture: tokenization layer, attention layer, transformer block, output layer and everything in between
(e) Pre-train the entire SLM
(f) Run inference and generate new text from your trained SLM!

This is not a toy project.

It's a production-level project with an extensive dataset.

These 3 hours will train the next generation of foundational LLM/AI engineers.

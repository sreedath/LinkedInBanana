When we released the “Build LLMs from scratch” Youtube series, it helped a lot of students completely understand LLM architecture from the ground up.

However, there are a number of students, researchers and industry professionals who are actively working on examples such as below:

(a) How to use LLMs to create a chatbot?
(b) Is fine-tuning better or should I do RAG?
(c) How to use LLMs to summarize sales calls and create actionable insights?
(d) How to create embedding models for customer sentiment analysis?
(e) How to integrate LLMs into your D2C product pipeline?

For these people, along with understanding how to build LLMs from scratch, they should also learn how to apply LLMs practically.

I received a PhD in Machine Learning from MIT in 2022.

I started a project to teach “Hands on Large Language Models”. The goal is to empower students, researchers and industry professionals to understand the practical aspects of deploying LLMs.

The result is a mega-project with videos and hands on projects covering everything about using LLMs practically. I have uploaded all videos on Youtube.

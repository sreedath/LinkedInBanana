Over the past few weeks, I have been thinking a lot about something that used to be an integral part of my life during my PhD at MIT - reading research papers line by line, from the first page to the very last reference. That habit used to give me a deep sense of satisfaction, not just because I learned new techniques or models, but because it made me feel like I was standing at the edge of human knowledge, watching how ideas evolve and connect across time.

After starting Vizuara Technologies Private Limited AI Labs, that habit slowly took a backseat. Running a company, teaching, and managing multiple projects left very little room for that slow, focused kind of reading. But recently, I have felt a strong pull to return to it. Not as an academic requirement, but as a personal practice - a way to think better, learn deeply, and stay connected to the roots of knowledge.

So, I have started a new video series on our channel: https://lnkd.in/g6nfHzB3

It is not a structured course, nor does it have fixed lectures or slides. It is simply a raw documentation of my process of reading research papers in full, line by line, without skipping anything or simplifying it into PowerPoint slides. The idea is to bring back the habit of reading difficult, technical papers directly from their source, rather than relying only on summaries, blog posts, or short videos.

In each video, I will select a paper - sometimes an old classic, sometimes a recent one - and read it from start to finish. It may take hours, but that is fine, because the goal is not speed. The goal is to understand.

If you have ever wanted to develop the habit of reading research papers, or if you have struggled to make sense of dense technical writing, this series might help. You can read along, pause, take notes, and most importantly, build the patience and curiosity that this process demands.

I do not expect many views. This is not meant to trend. It is simply my way of returning to something that once gave me immense joy and clarity.

If you wish to follow along, the first video is now live on our channel. Let us see if we can build a small community of people who genuinely enjoy reading papers and thinking deeply.

## Article Structure

The post must follow this exact four-section structure:

### 1. Opening Hook (1 paragraph, 2 to 3 sentences)

Acknowledge a relatable pain point or common perception about the topic covered in this playlist. Then immediately reframe it with a "but actually, the core idea is accessible" pivot. The reader should feel they are about to understand something they previously found intimidating or confusing. Do not open with a definition. Do not open with a question.

Pattern: "[Topic] looks [intimidating/complex] because [specific reasons]. However, at its core, [topic] is built on [simple idea], and once that clicks, the rest starts making sense."

### 2. Numbered Body Sections (3 to 5 sections, 3 to 6 sentences each)

Each numbered section carries exactly one idea or one logical step. The sections must follow a progressive construction pattern where each builds directly on the previous. Use the actual video titles and topics from the playlist data to make each section specific and concrete.

Weave the individual YouTube video links naturally into the body sections. When referencing a specific video topic, include its link inline. For example: "The forward diffusion process (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID) starts by..." Do not dump all links at the end. Spread them across the body sections where they are relevant.

Rules for numbered sections:
- First sentence states what this step is about
- Rest of the sentences explain, clarify, or give intuition
- Use plain language to explain technical ideas
- Do not front-load jargon; introduce a term only after the concept is clear
- Each section should feel like the natural next question after the previous one

### 3. Closing Distillation (1 paragraph, 2 to 4 sentences)

Distill the entire post into a single memorable insight. Start with a framing phrase like "If I had to explain [topic] in one line" or "The core insight is this." Follow with one sentence that reframes the reader's perspective.

### 4. Call to Action (1 to 2 sentences)

Mention Vizuara naturally as the team that created this playlist content. Include the playlist link. Invite the reader to watch, follow, or share their thoughts.

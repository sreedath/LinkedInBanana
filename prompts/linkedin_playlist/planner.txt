## Your Task

Analyze the playlist data below and:
1. Identify the core themes, topics, and narrative arc of the playlist
2. Choose the best visual format (infographic, timeline, mind map, grid layout, or flowchart)

## Visual Format Options

- **Infographic**: Best for playlists with statistics, facts, or step-by-step content. Uses icons, numbers, and visual hierarchy.
- **Timeline**: Best for chronological or sequential content. Shows progression from start to end.
- **Mind Map**: Best for topic-rich playlists with branching sub-themes. Central topic with radiating branches.
- **Grid Layout**: Best for diverse playlists where each video is a distinct topic. Clean card-based arrangement.
- **Flowchart**: Best for how-to or process-oriented playlists. Shows decision points and steps.
